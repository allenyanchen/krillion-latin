Puffer

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Puffer is a free, student-made website for practicing Latin with multiplayer games. This page explains what information the site uses and why.

If you don’t sign in

You can join and play games without an account. Your display name, character, stats and any sets you create are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They never leave your device except as described under “During a game” below.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email. When you do, we store:

  • Account details from Google or your email sign-in: your email address and name (and, for Google, the profile picture link Google provides).
  • Your profile: the display name and character look you choose, and simple game stats (games played, wins, correct answers).
  • Your sets: the question sets you create.

We use this only to sign you in and keep your sets, character and stats available on any device. Database rules make sure each person can only see and change their own information.

During a game

While you’re in a game room, your display name, character, answers, throws and chat messages are sent live to the other players in that room. Game rooms and chat are not saved; they disappear when the game ends.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your information.
  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use analytics or tracking cookies.

Services we use

  • Supabase: accounts, the database and live game connections.
  • Google: “Sign in with Google”.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: load fonts and code, so they can see your IP address when the page loads.

Deleting your information

Signed-out data can be cleared at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser. To delete your account and everything saved with it, contact us through the project page below and we’ll remove it.

Students

Puffer is meant for classroom practice. Teachers and students should follow their school’s rules about using online tools.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top. Questions or deletion requests: github.com/allenyanchen/krillion-latin/issues.
